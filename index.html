Identities恆等式 Identities

0Identity or equation?恆等式還是方程？

An identity (≡) is true for every value of the letters.恆等式 (identity, ≡) 對字母的所有數值都成立。

An equation (=) is true only for some values — here only x = 3.方程 (equation, =) 只對某些數值成立 — 這裏只有 x = 3。

1Why (a + b)² has a 2ab為甚麼 (a + b)² 有 2ab

Draw a square with side a + b. Its area is (a + b)². Split it and count the pieces.畫一個邊長為 a + b 的正方形，面積是 (a + b)²。把它分割，數一數有甚麼部分。

a =3
b =2

2Prove it by expanding用展開來證明

Multiply every term in the first bracket by every term in the second. Pick an identity and watch the table fill.把第一個括號的每一項乘以第二個括號的每一項。選一條恆等式，看看表格怎樣填滿。

3(a − b)²: cut two strips off(a − b)²：剪去兩條長條

Start with an a × a square and cut away strips of width b until a square of side a − b is left.由 a × a 的正方形開始，剪去闊 b 的長條，直至剩下邊長 a − b 的正方形。

4a² − b²: cut and slidea² − b²：剪開再移動

Cut a small b × b square out of an a × a square. Rearrange what is left into one rectangle.從 a × a 的正方形剪去一個 b × b 的小正方形，再把剩下的部分拼成一個長方形。

5The three identities三條恆等式

首平方，尾平方，首尾相乘兩倍放中央 — square the first, square the last, twice their product in the middle.

6Which identity? Or none?用哪一條？還是都不適用？

Expanding展開 (expand)

  1. One bracket, squared? → perfect square. Check the sign inside.一個括號的平方？→ 完全平方 (perfect square)。看括號內的符號。
  2. Two brackets, same two terms, one + and one −? → difference of two squares.兩個括號，兩項相同，一個 + 一個 −？→ 平方差 (difference of two squares)。
  3. Anything else → no identity. Expand term by term.其他 → 不適用，逐項展開。

Factorising (identity backwards)因式分解 (factorise)：倒轉使用恆等式

  1. 3 terms, first and last are squares, middle = ±2ab → perfect square.三項，首尾都是平方，中間項 = ±2ab → 完全平方。
  2. square − square → difference of two squares.平方 − 平方 → 平方差。
  3. square + square → cannot be factorised.平方 + 平方 → 不能因式分解。

7Test it with numbers代入數字驗證

An identity works for all values. One pair of numbers that fails is enough to prove a statement is not an identity.恆等式對所有數值都成立。只要找到一組數令等式不成立，就證明它不是恆等式。
